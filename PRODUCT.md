# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Small-business owners, agency leads, and marketing teams looking for a dependable remote specialist for website maintenance, responsive fixes, design production, landing pages, and email work. They are often evaluating Bilal quickly and need to understand his range, experience, reliability, and working model within a few seconds.

## Product Purpose

Present Bilal as an experienced, practical digital partner and turn qualified visitors into project enquiries. The portfolio should prove capability through polished presentation and clear information architecture while making services, support plans, process, and contact options easy to understand.

## Brand Personality

Assured, precise, and dependable. The experience should feel quietly ambitious and highly crafted, with a calm professional tone rather than hype. Visitors should feel confidence, clarity, and momentum.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS templates, dashboard-like layouts, excessive glassmorphism, neon futurism, purple AI gradients, oversized decorative blobs, endless identical cards, fake case studies, invented testimonials, aggressive parallax, and motion that delays access to content.

## Design Principles

1. Prove experience through craft: the interface itself demonstrates Bilal's design and frontend judgment.
2. Make the offer obvious: visitors can understand who Bilal is, what he does, and how to hire him within five seconds.
3. Build trust without fabrication: use real experience, transparent scope, and reliable-process signals rather than invented results.
4. Create rhythm, not noise: editorial composition, contrast, and purposeful motion guide the page without competing with the content.
5. Design for enquiry: every major journey leads naturally toward a clear, low-friction contact action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic landmarks, sufficient contrast, touch-friendly controls, meaningful alternative text, and full usability with reduced motion enabled. Hover interactions must have equivalent persistent or focus-accessible content.
